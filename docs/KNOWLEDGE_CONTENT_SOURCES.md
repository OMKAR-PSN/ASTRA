# Crew Knowledge Hub — content source register

Feature 06. Initial content reviewed 2026-11-17. All knowledge cards are original plain-language
summaries. They are not copied passages, not NASA-approved flight medical guidance, and not a
substitute for approved mission medical procedures or a designated medical officer.

## Sourcing rules used for this catalog

- Prefer primary or official public sources: NASA Human Research Program, NASA technical
  standards and handbooks, the Canadian Space Agency, and public-health education pages from
  MedlinePlus, NHLBI, CDC and NCCIH.
- Describe general concepts, not clinical reference ranges. No source is used to create a
  diagnosis, triage rule, medication instruction or treatment procedure.
- Record the source title, publisher, URL, publication date where the publisher states one,
  review date, topic and content version.
- If a page did not state a publication date, the register says "Not stated" rather than
  guessing. Links were opened during review; no unverified source is cited.
- External links require internet. Card text and citations are bundled with the app and work
  offline.

## Review notes

- NASA HRP pages describe research themes, not individual clinical thresholds. Cards repeat
  that distinction.
- The Mars communications delay figure is stated in S11 as a maximum one-way delay of about
  21–22 minutes; the card presents it as an approximate published figure, not a live link
  status.
- Public-health sleep guidance is general population education. The card does not convert it
  into a mission-specific requirement or a sleep-disorder assessment.
- NASA-STD-3001 is listed for scope awareness only. No card quotes or paraphrases it as
  operational medical direction.
- Content version 1.0 is the initial bundled catalog. Card-level `contentVersion` values are
  independent of this register version.
